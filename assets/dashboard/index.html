        Exactly what may Agent Control inspect?

        Select sources explicitly. No sources are preselected. FULL DIAGNOSTIC SCAN still obeys all limits; it never means unrestricted access. Grants last one hour and are revocable.

        Collection limits: 2 MiB total, 32 selected sources, 2,000 lines per source, 500 event groups, 10 seconds per read helper. RECENT SAMPLE: at most 24 hours. Other scans: at most seven days. Unknown timestamps are excluded.

        Privacy and optional model analysis

        Secrets are always redacted heuristically. Identifier handling is configurable above. Local-only model calls require a qualified route to a numeric loopback endpoint. Models cannot invoke tools or choose confidence. Unexpected secrets may evade heuristics.

# Tsunora brand

**Connect work to capability.** The operating system for digital work. **Powered by Agent Control.**

Tsunora is a coined brand, inspired in part by tsunagu (connecting/linking); it is not claimed to be a Japanese word. It continues the creator's KNect Solutions philosophy of connecting people, process, technology and solutions. This provenance belongs here, not throughout the operating UI.

The junction mark has independent paths meeting a precise central connection. Flat silver strokes and a restrained vermilion junction work without effects. The interface uses charcoal, graphite, silver and warm ivory with generous spacing. No photographic dependency, gradients, robot imagery or operational widgets appear in the hero. Final photorealistic metal treatment is deferred; no raster is represented as a production vector.

Use the monochrome SVG for single-colour reproduction; the compact icon retains the junction at small sizes. The wordmark has a system-font dependency and should be outlined before a final print brand release. Asset review and trademark clearance are not supplied by this technical fork.

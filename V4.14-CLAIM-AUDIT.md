# RC2 claim audit rules

The sealed final report binds actual results to the exact candidate and archive. Public notes describe bounded implementation and explicitly refer to that report; RC1 numbers are not final RC2 evidence.

"Out of box" requires the pristine-install gate. "Rollback" requires the new-format operational round trip. "Reliable" is restricted to the frozen workload. "Safe repair" means no false repairs in its corpus and no authority/schema invention. "No progress" distinguishes completed tool loops from provider generation. "Security" remains PARTIAL. "Live evidence" requires an attached recorder and verification. "Multi-host" does not establish Pixel identity. "Qualified" applies only to named gates, never all models or platforms.

AutomationBench is excluded and unqualified. Strands is excluded; AWS cost reproduction is unproven. Atomic multi-file operations are deferred. No merge, tag, publication, deployment or upgrade of a running installation is authorised by this document.
